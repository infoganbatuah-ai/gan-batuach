# Final Product Roadmap Completion Report

## Status

PHASE 190 completes the numbered product-building roadmap and transitions Gan Batuach and Digital Observer into company operations.

This report does not mean the products are publicly launched. Public launch still requires explicit human approval, external validation evidence, provider activation approval and closure of critical blockers.

## Completed Systems

- Gan Batuach launch readiness, commercial rollout, 100-kindergarten scale readiness and national inspection workforce operations.
- Digital Observer standalone product shell, marketing funnel, package/billing journey, pilot, paid beta, separation readiness and commercial launch readiness.
- Provider production activation readiness for email, WhatsApp, SMS, push, payments, invoices, camera gateway, AI and webhooks.
- Mobile submission readiness for App Store, TestFlight, Google Play and Google internal testing.
- External validation execution framework for privacy/legal review, camera compliance, AI governance, penetration testing, ISO and provider compliance.
- Final production command center and company operating dashboard.

## Current Recommendation

Recommended decision: `pilot_only`

Reason:

- The platform has broad internal readiness.
- External legal/privacy/security validation is still required.
- Live provider activation must be approved provider-by-provider.
- Mobile store submission remains a manual external step.
- Digital Observer can continue controlled beta/soft-launch preparation, but standalone production launch still requires domain/provider decisions.

## Remaining External Dependencies

- Privacy lawyer review and written feedback.
- Camera compliance review.
- AI governance legal review for restricted capabilities.
- External penetration test and retest.
- ISO consultant and certification body process.
- App Store and Google Play account/signing/submission workflow.
- Payment and invoice provider production approval.
- Vercel/domain/DNS setup for Digital Observer if standalone domain is selected.

## Operating Model After Phase 190

Stop creating large numbered phases.

Move to:

- Monthly releases
- Weekly support and customer-success review
- Monthly security and provider review
- Monthly AI/camera calibration review
- Mobile release cycle when store changes are ready
- Quarterly compliance and external-review refresh
- Customer feedback triage into `product_roadmap_items`

## Launch Guardrails

Do not claim:

- ISO certified
- legally approved
- regulator approved
- guaranteed safety
- security certified

Allowed wording before external confirmation:

- ISO readiness
- external review in progress
- security controls implemented
- privacy-by-design architecture
- compliance readiness
