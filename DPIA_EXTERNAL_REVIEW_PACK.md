# DPIA External Review Pack

**DRAFT FOR LEGAL REVIEW**

## System Purpose

Gan Batuach supports kindergarten operations, safety readiness, parent transparency, inspections, staff readiness, payments, communications and reviewed AI observer signals.

## Affected Users

- children
- parents
- staff
- managers
- inspectors
- admins

## Data Categories

- child records
- medical and allergy data
- parent contact and identity data
- staff certificates and clearance readiness
- attendance and pickup events
- camera access logs
- AI / skeleton metadata
- documents and inspection evidence
- billing metadata

## Main Risks

- excessive parent visibility
- camera misuse
- AI output mistaken for decision
- sensitive medical exposure
- cross-garden access
- over-retention
- child identity inference

## Mitigations

- human review mandatory
- raw AI blocked from parents
- no audio/face recognition in Gan Batuach
- private storage and audit logs
- MFA readiness for sensitive actions
- retention and legal hold workflows
- capability matrix by vertical

- Which processing purposes need additional justification?

- Which retention periods are defensible?
