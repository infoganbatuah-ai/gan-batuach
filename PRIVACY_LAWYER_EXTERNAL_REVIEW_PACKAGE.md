# Privacy Lawyer External Review Package

Status: package for external privacy lawyer review. Not legal approval.

## Review Scope

- privacy policy draft
- data processing agreement readiness
- data flow mapping
- DPIA
- data subject rights workflow
- deletion/anonymization workflow
- retention policies
- parent visibility rules
- staff privacy rules
- child data protection model
- medical data encryption model

## Key Questions

- Are parent, child, staff and inspector notices sufficient?
- Are controller/processor roles described correctly?
- Are deletion, anonymization and legal hold rules acceptable?
- Are medical data controls sufficient?
- Are camera and AI boundaries clear enough for parents and staff?
- Are subprocessors, DPAs and international transfer concerns handled?

## Restrictions

External reviewers must not receive raw child records, medical records, live camera streams, payment details, secrets or raw AI events unless separately approved through a secure access process.
