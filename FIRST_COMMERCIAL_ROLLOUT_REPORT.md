# First Commercial Rollout Report

Status: template and baseline report for the first 10-25 kindergarten rollout.

## Cohort Summary

Target cohort size: 10-25 kindergartens.

Current baseline recommendation: continue stabilization before expanding to 50 kindergartens.

## Customers Onboarded

Track for each customer:

- kindergarten name
- source
- city
- onboarding status
- payment status
- support owner
- customer health score
- risk level

## Revenue

Track:

- expected MRR
- collected MRR
- failed payments
- unpaid balances
- discounts used
- average revenue per kindergarten
- revenue per class/age group

Revenue split:

- Gan Batuach subscription routes to Gan Batuach company account
- parent tuition routes to kindergarten account/provider
