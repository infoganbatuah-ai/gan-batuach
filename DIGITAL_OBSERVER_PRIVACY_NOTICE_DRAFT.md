# Digital Observer Privacy Notice Draft

**DRAFT FOR LEGAL REVIEW**

This document is a privacy notice readiness draft for external review. It is not a final privacy policy.

## Data Processed

Digital Observer may process:

- site owner contact details
- site metadata
- camera connection metadata
- observer events
- alert delivery metadata
- billing metadata
- support records
- audit logs
- usage and product analytics

Camera credentials and RTSP URLs must remain server-side and must not be exposed to browsers.

## Camera Data Handling

The paid beta is designed around controlled camera connection, gateway registration, secure playback tokens and audit logs.

Raw camera streams are not used for unrestricted client-side processing. Recording and retention depend on package, provider readiness and customer configuration.

## Observer Events

Observer events are generated to support monitoring and review. They do not create automatic accusations, final conclusions, emergency actions or enforcement decisions.

Sensitive capabilities are controlled by the capability matrix and may require legal review or consent before activation.

## Alerts

Alerts may be delivered through:

- in-app
- email
- SMS readiness
- WhatsApp readiness
- push readiness

Real delivery depends on provider configuration and user preferences.

## Billing Data

Digital Observer billing is separate from Gan Batuach kindergarten billing and parent tuition payments.

No raw payment card data may be stored.

## Audit Logs

Administrative, billing, camera, gateway, alert review and support actions should be auditable.

Audit logs must not contain camera credentials, RTSP URLs, payment card data or unnecessary private content.

## Retention And Deletion

Retention periods depend on package, legal requirements, audit needs and support obligations.

Deletion/anonymization requests should connect to the privacy request workflow and may require admin review where legal holds or evidence retention apply.

## Review Required

This draft requires review by privacy counsel before publication or customer use.
