- public website and app gateway

- role dashboards for parent, manager, staff, inspector and admin

## Demo Rules

- Use synthetic data only.
- Use demo accounts only.
- Do not enter real child, parent, staff, medical, payment or document data.
- Do not claim live camera/AI/payment.
- Do not share beyond the approved controlled audience.

## Reporting Issues
