# Final Platform Documentation Index

This index is the handoff map for Gan Batuach and Digital Observer after PHASE 190.

Important guardrails:

- Do not claim ISO certification, legal approval, regulatory approval or security certification unless an authorized external body has issued evidence.
- Do not launch publicly without human go/no-go approval.
- Do not mix Gan Batuach subscriptions, parent-to-kindergarten tuition payments and Digital Observer subscriptions.
- Do not expose secrets, service keys, RTSP URLs, camera credentials, child data, medical data or raw AI events.

## Operating Cadence After Phase 190

After PHASE 190, the team should stop using large numbered phases and move to:

- Monthly product releases
- Customer-driven backlog triage
- Security patch cycles
- Provider update cycles
- AI calibration cycles
- Camera/gateway improvement cycles
- Mobile release cycles
- Compliance and external-review cycles
- Support and sales operations reviews
