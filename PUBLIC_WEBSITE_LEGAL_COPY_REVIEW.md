# Public Website Legal Copy Review

**DRAFT FOR LEGAL REVIEW**

## Risky Claims To Avoid

- AI prevents violence.
- AI identifies children.
- AI detects abuse.
- Gan Batuach is legally compliant.
- Gan Batuach is ISO certified.
- Parents have unrestricted camera access.
- The system guarantees child safety.

## Safer Wording

- supports safety monitoring
- assists human review
- helps identify situations requiring attention
- improves transparency
- ISO readiness
- compliance readiness
- controlled parent viewing where enabled and legally permitted

## Parent Marketing Guardrails

- no panic marketing
- no unsupported safety guarantee
- no raw AI visibility promise
- no unrestricted camera promise
- no automatic accusation wording
