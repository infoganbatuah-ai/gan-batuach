## Required Topics

- processing purposes

- retention and deletion
- legal holds
- camera viewing controls
- AI human review boundaries
- privacy rights
- contact and complaint channels

## Legal Review Notes

Every notice should clearly distinguish operational data, camera metadata, approved summaries and internal observer signals.
