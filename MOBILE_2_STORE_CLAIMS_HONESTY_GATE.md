# MOBILE 2 - Store Claims Honesty Gate

Date: 2026-06-27

## Allowed Store Claims

Allowed with cautious wording:

- Gan Batuach supports kindergarten management, supervision workflows, documentation, and communication.
- The app includes role-based experiences for parents, kindergarten managers, staff, inspectors, and platform admins.
- The app supports enrollment, child profile workflows, attendance, documents, messages, inspections, reports, and readiness states.
- Payment, camera, AI, and push modules are subject to provider configuration, policy approval, and pilot readiness.
- Camera/AI modules are readiness/shadow/internal-review capabilities unless real gateway/inference was explicitly validated.

## Claims Not Allowed Yet

Do not claim:

- live AI protection
- real-time abuse or violence detection
- automatic detection of neglect or harm
- guaranteed child safety
- official government or regulator approval
- ISO/legal certification
- live parent camera viewing
- live payments
- biometric or face recognition for Gan Batuach Israel Mode
- production-ready camera/AI unless validated in the relevant production phases

## Recommended Wording

Use:

- “supports safety workflows”
- “helps organize supervision and documentation”
- “enables internal review processes”
- “camera and AI modules are subject to configuration, policy approval, and human review”
- “pilot/demo mode where applicable”
