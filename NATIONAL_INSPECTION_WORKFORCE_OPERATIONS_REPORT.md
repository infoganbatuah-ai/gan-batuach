# National Inspection Workforce Operations Report

Status: initial workforce readiness report. Human inspectors remain mandatory.

## Executive Summary

Gan Batuach now has a national inspection workforce operating model for scaling inspector operations beyond individual inspection records.

Baseline workforce readiness score: 53/100.

Recommended state: workforce preparation required before 100-kindergarten scale can be considered operationally stable.

## Active Inspectors

The system now tracks inspector workforce profiles separately from basic user profiles.

Tracked fields:

- active status
- region
- city coverage
- monthly capacity
- assigned kindergarten count
- completed inspections
- overdue inspections
- certification status
- training status

## Coverage

Regional assignments now support:

- primary inspector
- backup inspector
- regional supervisor readiness
- region/city/network/manual assignment
- workload balancing

Known gap:

- real regional coverage needs actual inspector assignments and city mapping.

## Workload

The monthly workload planner tracks:

- due inspections
- scheduled inspections
- overdue inspections
- urgent visits
- follow-ups
- complaint-driven inspections
- unassigned kindergartens
- overloaded inspectors

Baseline risk:

- 100 kindergartens require roughly six inspectors or a route-optimized equivalent.

## Cost

The compensation model supports:

- 50 NIS/month per assigned kindergarten
- per-inspection pay
- fixed salary
- hybrid model

This report uses compensation estimates only. No payout execution was added.

## Financial Impact

Forecast scenarios now cover:

- 25 kindergartens
- 50 kindergartens
- 100 kindergartens
- 250 kindergartens
- 500 kindergartens
- 1,000 kindergartens

The model connects inspector cost to expected subscription revenue, support cost, infrastructure cost and contribution margin.

## SLA Breaches

SLA readiness now covers:

- monthly inspection completion
- complaint response
- urgent inspection response
- report submission
- corrective action follow-up

No real breach count is validated until production inspection operations feed the model.

## Performance

Inspector performance score includes:

- inspections assigned
- inspections completed
- overdue inspections
- completion time
- GPS validation rate
- report quality
- corrective action follow-up
- complaint handling
- manager feedback
- admin review

Performance data should be used for coaching and quality assurance, not automatic employment or regulatory action.

## Training Recommendations

Required modules:

- Gan Batuach inspection standard
- child safety basics
- documentation requirements
- GPS validation
- digital forms
- evidence upload
- complaint handling
- privacy and confidentiality
- camera/AI observer boundaries
- professional conduct

Training completion should be mandatory before an inspector is marked active.

## Certification Gaps

Required documents:

- ID verification
- background check readiness
- training completion
- confidentiality agreement
- service agreement
- professional certification if relevant
- insurance/contractor documents if relevant

No inspector should be fully activated before mandatory documents are approved.

## Regional Gaps

Initial expected gaps:

- Center region inspector coverage must be validated.
- Sharon region needs candidate pipeline.
- Future scale requires supervisor readiness.
- Travel assumptions require real route validation.

## Risk Register

Current high-priority risks:

- inspector hiring lag
- regional coverage gaps
- inspection quality issues
- SLA breaches
- observer recommendations being mistaken for automatic decisions

Mitigation:

- use the capacity model before every scale cohort
- assign backup inspectors
- require quality reviews
- keep human review mandatory

## Next Scale Recommendation

Before expanding inspection operations:

- activate real inspector workforce profiles
- assign primary and backup inspectors to active kindergartens
- validate the 100-kindergarten capacity forecast
- approve compensation model
- complete training and certification workflows
- measure SLA adherence for one monthly cycle
- keep Observer/AI as recommendation-only

The system is ready for workforce operations tracking, but not yet proven as a national workforce in production.
