# External Legal, Privacy And Regulatory Review Pack

**DRAFT FOR LEGAL REVIEW**

This is the master package for external review. It does not grant legal approval.

## Review Workflow

1. Assign legal review item.
2. External reviewer reviews pack and notes.
3. Required changes are logged.
4. Implementation is completed.
5. Re-review is requested.
6. Decision is recorded: approved, approved with conditions, rejected or blocked.

## External Reviewer Checklist

- Privacy lawyer: child data, camera notices, AI/DPIA, retention, rights.
- Regulatory lawyer: kindergarten obligations, camera legality, controller filing.
- ISO consultant: evidence, policies, controls and audit readiness.
- Camera compliance expert: streaming, anti-leak, parent viewing and logs.
- Payment/accounting reviewer: revenue separation and invoices.
- Penetration tester: auth, API, storage, camera and payment routes.

## Open Legal Questions

- Parent live camera viewing policy.
- Web screenshot limitation disclosure.
- DPIA approval for production observer mode.
- Controller / processor role split.
- Database registration or notification duties.
- Retention periods for child, medical, camera, AI and audit records.

## Launch Boundary

Gan Batuach may be technically readiness-tracked, but production launch still requires external legal/privacy review, security review, payment review and operational approval.
