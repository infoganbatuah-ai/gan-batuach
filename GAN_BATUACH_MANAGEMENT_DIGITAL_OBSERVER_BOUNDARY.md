## DIGITAL OBSERVER INTEGRATION REQUIREMENTS

1. Versioned capability truth contract with UI/contract/provider/production/provenance states.
2. Server-to-server short-lived signed playback/evidence issuance after Management authorization.
3. Reviewed-event feed with provenance, lifecycle and signed evidence; mock/shadow exclusion.
4. Cross-product request context containing actor, role, garden, purpose and TTL without inherited parent access.

Do not rebuild camera, event, evidence, risk, verification, identity, watch-rule or investigation engines in Management.
