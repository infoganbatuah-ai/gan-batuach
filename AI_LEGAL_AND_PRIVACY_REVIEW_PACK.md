# AI Legal And Privacy Review Pack

**DRAFT FOR LEGAL REVIEW**

## AI Role

AI is an assistant. It may recommend, prioritize and surface signals for human review.

AI may not:

- accuse
- discipline
- punish
- make legal decisions
- make regulatory decisions
- notify parents automatically about raw detections

## Governance Controls

- capability matrix by vertical
- Gan Batuach Israel Mode
- DPIA readiness
- model registry and version tracking
- explainability readiness
- human review queue
- AI decision audit trail
- parent visibility boundary

## Parent Visibility

Parents may see:

- approved summaries
- approved events
- manager-approved updates

Parents may not see:

- raw AI events
- raw observer signals
- unreviewed anomalies
- internal confidence scores
- investigation drafts
