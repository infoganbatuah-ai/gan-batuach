# Retention, Deletion And Legal Hold Review Pack

**DRAFT FOR LEGAL REVIEW**

## Review Scope

- parent deletion requests
- child deletion/anonymization
- medical data deletion
- audit log retention
- inspection retention
- incident evidence retention
- payment and invoice retention
- camera metadata retention
- AI telemetry retention

## Current Model

Deletion is reviewed before execution. Records may be blocked by:

- active enrollment
- unpaid balances
- active incident
- complaint
- inspection evidence
- legal hold
- retention obligation
- audit requirement

## Legal Hold Categories

- safety investigation
- inspection evidence
- complaint
- payment dispute
- legal dispute
- regulatory review
