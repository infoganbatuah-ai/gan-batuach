# AUTHED UX QA 1 - Inspector Results

## Result

`BLOCKED_BY_AUTH_ACCESS`

Inspector dashboards were not accepted because safe authenticated Inspector sessions were not established.

## Expected Scope

- Pending/unassigned state
- Assigned gardens
- Inspection dashboard
- Inspection form
- Evidence upload readiness
- Findings
- History/report

## Blockers

- No verified unassigned inspector session.
- No verified assigned inspector session.
- No multi-role session switch completed.

## Pilot Impact

High for inspection-included pilot. Inspection flows cannot be considered visually/functionally accepted yet.
