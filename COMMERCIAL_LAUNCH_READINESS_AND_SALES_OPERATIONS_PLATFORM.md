# Commercial Launch Readiness And Sales Operations Platform

Status: commercial readiness only. This document does not approve final legal contracts, live payment activation, broad outbound messaging, or production sales commitments.

## Pricing Model

Gan Batuach Standard:

- Base package: 800 NIS/month.
- Includes one age group/class.
- Annual subscription paid monthly.
- Additional age group/class: 200 NIS/month.
- Pilot pricing requires explicit admin approval.
- Enterprise and network pricing require custom approval and contract review.

The core value is sold as one all-in-one package, not fragmented feature add-ons.

## Objection Handling

Answers should stay clear, practical, and non-technical.

Examples:

- “זה יקר”: explain all-in-one operational value and pilot measurement.
- “כבר יש לי מערכת”: position Gan Batuach as trust, compliance readiness, and operational layer, not only software.
- “הורים לא ירצו מצלמות”: explain that parent viewing is not default and requires policy, permissions, hours, audit, and legal/privacy approval.
- “מה עם פרטיות?”: explain controlled transparency and approved information only.
- “האם זה חוקי?”: state readiness for legal review, not legal certification.
