# Executive ISO Certification Readiness Report

Status: internal readiness report. This is not an ISO certificate and must not be used as a certification claim.

## Executive Summary

Gan Batuach has a structured ISO readiness foundation for ISO/IEC 27001, ISO/IEC 27017 and ISO/IEC 27701. The platform now includes control mapping, evidence tracking, policy/procedure readiness, supplier evidence tracking, risk treatment planning, internal audit readiness, management review readiness and a safe external reviewer handoff model.

Baseline readiness score: 61/100.

This score reflects internal preparation, not external validation.

## Standard Readiness

- ISO 27001: security management controls are partially prepared through access control, audit logging, risk register, incident response, asset inventory, change management and backup readiness.
- ISO 27017: cloud and SaaS controls are partially prepared through Supabase, Vercel, GitHub, tenant isolation, deployment controls and shared-responsibility documentation.
- ISO 27701: privacy controls are partially prepared through privacy requests, child data protection, DPIA readiness, AI privacy controls, camera privacy controls and retention workflows.

Gan Batuach may say:

- ISO readiness
- Certification preparation
- Compliance readiness
- Security controls implemented
- Privacy-by-design architecture

Gan Batuach must not say:

- ISO certified
- Privacy certified
- Legally approved
- Regulator approved

unless a qualified external party has issued written evidence.
