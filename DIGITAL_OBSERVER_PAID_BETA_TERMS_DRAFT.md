# Digital Observer Paid Beta Terms Draft

**DRAFT FOR LEGAL REVIEW**

This document is a working draft for external legal review. It is not final legal advice, not a customer-ready contract, and not a launch approval.

## Purpose

The paid beta validates Digital Observer as a standalone camera monitoring product for homes, offices, businesses, warehouses, stores, parking lots and custom monitored sites.

Digital Observer is separate from:

- Gan Batuach kindergarten subscriptions
- parent tuition payments
- kindergarten payout configuration

## Beta Limitations

Digital Observer paid beta is provided in a controlled evaluation mode. Some capabilities may be unavailable, limited, or subject to manual review.

The system does not guarantee prevention of incidents, does not replace human judgment, and does not create automatic safety, security, disciplinary or emergency conclusions.

## Camera Owner Responsibility

The customer is responsible for:

- owning or being authorized to connect cameras
- confirming that camera use is lawful at the site
- posting notices where required
- obtaining consents where required
- maintaining camera hardware, networking and DVR/NVR access
- avoiding prohibited surveillance uses

## Capability Restrictions

Sensitive capabilities such as face recognition, audio analytics, gait analytics, person identity matching or biometric profiling are not enabled automatically.

Capabilities may be:

- allowed
- disabled
- legal_review_required
- consent_required
- future_only

## Payment Terms Readiness

Live charging is allowed only when the configured payment provider is explicitly in live mode.

No raw payment card data may be stored by Digital Observer.

Invoices for Digital Observer are separate from Gan Batuach and parent tuition invoices.

## Cancellation

Customers may request cancellation during beta. If a subscription is cancelled or suspended:

- monitoring may be paused
- billing/support access may remain available
- historical data remains governed by retention policy

## Support Scope

Paid beta support covers:

- onboarding
- camera connection guidance
- gateway setup readiness
- alert calibration
- billing readiness
- support issue triage

This draft requires external legal review before commercial use.
