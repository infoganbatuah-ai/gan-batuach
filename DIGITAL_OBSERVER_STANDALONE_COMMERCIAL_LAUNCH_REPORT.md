# Digital Observer Standalone Commercial Launch Report

## Launch Status

Current recommendation:

**Controlled soft launch readiness only.**

Digital Observer should not be marked commercially live until launch blockers are closed, legal/capability review is complete, and live billing is explicitly configured.

## Readiness Score

Initial readiness seed:

64/100

This reflects:

- public website readiness
- package readiness
- lead capture readiness
- route-only product shell readiness
- paid beta readiness
- remaining gaps in live billing, legal/capability review, camera gateway scale and support evidence

## Active Blockers

Commercial launch remains blocked by:

- live billing not enabled
- legal/capability review still required
- camera gateway scale not proven
- no automatic activation of restricted capabilities
- need for QA completion before public paid launch

## Package Readiness

Launch packages:

- Home Basic
- Home Plus
- Business Basic
- Business Pro
- Enterprise Monitoring

Prices should remain readiness values, “starting from”, or “contact us” unless finalized by admin and provider configuration.

## Billing Readiness

Digital Observer billing must remain separate from:

- Gan Batuach kindergarten subscriptions
- parent tuition payments
- kindergarten payout configuration

No raw card data may be stored.

## Camera Readiness

Commercial launch requires:

- gateway playback readiness
- RTSP hidden from client
- credentials hidden from client
- short-lived viewing tokens
- audit logging
- camera health checks

## Support Readiness

Support playbooks must cover:

- camera setup failed
- RTSP unknown
- DVR/NVR channel issue
- gateway unavailable
- playback issue
- alerts too noisy
- missed alert
- billing issue
- package upgrade
- cancellation request

## Legal / Capability Status

Restricted capabilities cannot be active unless explicitly approved.

Examples requiring review:

- face recognition
- audio analytics
- gait recognition
- identity matching
- soft biometrics

## Go / No-Go Recommendation

Recommendation:

**Soft launch only, with admin-controlled onboarding and provider-gated billing.**

Do not move to full commercial launch until:

- paid customer evidence improves
- QA is completed
- live billing is configured
- camera/gateway support load is understood
- legal and capability reviews are complete
