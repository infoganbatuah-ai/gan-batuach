# First Commercial Rollout, 10-25 Kindergarten Launch And Revenue Validation

Status: controlled commercial rollout readiness. This is not a mass launch plan.

## Goal

Validate Gan Batuach with the first real commercial cohort of 10-25 kindergartens.

The rollout validates:

- real customer onboarding
- payment readiness
- parent adoption
- staff adoption
- manager adoption
- inspection operations
- support capacity
- revenue validation
- business model validation

## Rollout Model

The first commercial rollout is managed as a cohort, not as open-ended growth.

Rules:

- do not onboard hundreds of kindergartens
- do not skip legal/security/privacy blockers
- do not activate unvalidated camera/AI parent visibility
- do not route parent tuition to Gan Batuach
- do not mark scale-ready until the cohort meets success criteria

## Cohort Model

Each cohort tracks:

- cohort name
- target kindergarten count
- start and end dates
- status
- owner
- success criteria

Allowed cohort size:

- minimum: 10 kindergartens
- maximum: 25 kindergartens

Statuses:

- planned
- recruiting
- onboarding
- active
- stabilizing
- completed
- paused

## Commercial Activation Checklist

Each kindergarten must complete:

- contract or terms accepted
- subscription configured
- payment method set
- invoice details completed
- manager onboarded
- staff invited
- parents invited
- documents uploaded
- pricing configured
- first inspection scheduled
- support contact assigned

## Revenue Validation

Gan Batuach subscription:

- annual agreement
- monthly charge
- base 800 NIS/month
- 200 NIS/month per additional age group/class
- discount code readiness
- failed payment handling
- grace period
- suspension readiness after unpaid grace period

Parent tuition payments:

- parent pays kindergarten
- money routes to kindergarten account/provider
- Gan Batuach does not hold tuition funds
- manager can see payment status
- receipt/invoice readiness depends on provider and legal structure

Track:

- training needs
- satisfaction score

## Success Criteria

A cohort is successful only if:

- at least 80% managers complete onboarding
- at least 70% parents activate
- staff workflows are used weekly
- payment flow works
- no critical privacy/security issue is open
- support load is manageable
- customer satisfaction is acceptable
- renewal risk is low
- no critical blockers remain open

## Customer Health Score

Customer health is scored 0-100 using:

- manager usage
- parent activation
- staff activation
- payment status
- support issues
- compliance readiness
- inspection readiness
- satisfaction

## Support Staffing Forecast

Forecast bands:

- 25 gardens: named CS/support owner plus onboarding support
- 50 gardens: dedicated CS and support coverage
- 100 gardens: tiered support and training operations
- 500 gardens: support team, knowledge base and automation required

## Inspector Staffing Forecast

Assumptions:

- one inspection per kindergarten per month
- average inspection duration
- travel time
- follow-up inspection rate
- complaint-driven inspection rate

Forecast bands:

- 25 gardens
- 50 gardens
- 100 gardens
- 500 gardens

## Expansion Decision

After the cohort, admin may mark:

- not ready
- continue stabilization
- ready for 50 gardens
- ready for regional rollout
- ready for commercial scale

## Remaining Rollout Blockers

Before expansion:

- live payment validation must pass
- parent activation must approach target
- support repeat issues must be reduced
- legal/privacy/security blockers must remain zero critical
- first inspection cycle must run cleanly
- revenue split must be validated with provider evidence
