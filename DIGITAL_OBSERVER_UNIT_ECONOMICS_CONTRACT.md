# DIGITAL OBSERVER — UNIT ECONOMICS CONTRACT

Date: 2026-09-10

## Definitions

- Infrastructure cost: Digital Observer operating resource cost measured by PUSH 33.
- Customer price: commercial charge owned by PUSH 40.
- Unit economics: cost/revenue/margin relationship requiring both reconciled costs and real pricing/revenue evidence, completed later in PUSH 49.

PUSH 33 must not calculate gross margin or customer profitability.

## Supported future units

- infrastructure cost / expected active physical camera / hour or month
- AI, storage and bandwidth cost / camera / month
- cost / Site and tenant
- cost / Incident where attribution is meaningful

These units are publishable only when duration and denominator are supported. Empty/unassigned channels are excluded. Offline/recovering components may retain real shared costs, which must not be disguised as active-camera processing.

## Projection contract

Every projection states its source window, physical-camera denominator, scaling rule, rate assumptions and evidence classification. A linear 10/100/1,000-camera projection from a short Home sample is capacity-planning input only. It is not Production scale proof, a monthly quote or an acquisition-grade unit-economic result.

## Final proof boundary

Final Product unit economics requires sustained pilot/cohort telemetry, provider-bill reconciliation within an explicitly approved tolerance, real retention/bandwidth patterns, approved local-hardware assumptions, and actual customer price/revenue evidence. PUSH 49 owns that closure.
